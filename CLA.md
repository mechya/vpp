# Part B — Corporate Contributor License Agreement

## B1. Who this part is for

A company or other legal entity (**"the Corporation"**) signs Part B when its employees or contractors contribute to VPP as part of their work. The Corporation names the individuals authorised to contribute on its behalf in a schedule to this agreement and keeps that schedule current.

## B2. Terms

Sections A1 to A9 apply to the Corporation as if it were "You", for every Contribution submitted by the individuals named in its schedule. The Corporation also represents that each named individual is authorised to submit Contributions on its behalf.

Each named individual also accepts Part A through the CLA Assistant bot, so that each pull request can be checked.

## B3. How the Corporation signs

Part B is signed outside GitHub. Before its employees contribute, the Corporation contacts the Licensor at the commercial licensing contact in `LICENSE.md`, and receives this agreement with the schedule to sign. The signature must be made by a person with authority to bind the Corporation.
