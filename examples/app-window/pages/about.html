About

The site's vpp.json sets titleBar to false, cornerRadius to 12, and height to auto. The viewer draws no bar, rounds the window, and sizes it to this page, so the window is shorter here than on the home page.

The icons are Bootstrap Icons SVG files pasted in with vpp-include and filled by the engine's path renderer. The buttons call VPP.window.minimize(), maximize(), and close().

Ctrl+L always brings the viewer's bar back with the real address, and Esc hides it again.

App Window example, built with VPP. Icons: Bootstrap Icons, MIT.
