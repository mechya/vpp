# VPP Commercial Licensing Policy

**Project:** VPP — Viewer Package Platform  
**Commercial Licensing Contact:** gurungb323@gmail.com

This document describes the intended standard annual commercial pricing for VPP.

The VPP Source-Available License determines when a paid commercial license is required. This document sets the standard pricing.

## Pricing Basis

Commercial pricing is based primarily on the **Annual Gross Product Revenue of the specific Product or service using VPP**, not unrelated company revenue.

Example:

```text
Company annual revenue:          US$10,000,000
Product using VPP revenue:          US$400,000

Relevant VPP revenue:               US$400,000
```

## Standard Annual Pricing

| Annual Gross Product Revenue | Annual VPP Commercial License |
|---:|---:|
| Below US$100,000 | Free |
| US$100,000 – US$499,999 | US$1,000 / year |
| US$500,000 – US$1,999,999 | US$5,000 / year |
| US$2,000,000 – US$9,999,999 | US$15,000 / year |
| US$10,000,000 – US$49,999,999 | US$35,000 / year |
| US$50,000,000+ | Negotiated enterprise license |

## Separate Negotiation

Separate terms may be required for:
- enterprise-wide deployment;
- OEM or hardware embedding;
- operating-system distribution;
- white-label redistribution;
- preinstallation on devices;
- large-scale cloud/platform deployments;
- redistribution of VPP itself;
- custom support or SLA agreements.

## Sponsorship Is Separate

Donations, GitHub Sponsors payments, crowdfunding, and corporate sponsorships are voluntary.

They do not replace a commercial-license fee unless expressly agreed in writing.

Example:

```text
GitHub sponsorship:              US$25/month
Required commercial license:    US$5,000/year

The sponsorship does not replace the commercial license.
```

## Pricing Changes

The copyright holder may change these published standard prices prospectively.

Changes do not retroactively alter an already signed commercial agreement for its active term unless that agreement says otherwise.
